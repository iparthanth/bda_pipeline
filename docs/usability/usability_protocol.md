# Usability check with non-CS users (Visualisation Engineer, brief Section 7.1)

**Status: TO BE CARRIED OUT BY THE TEAM.** The brief asks for a usability check with at least two users who
are not computer scientists (e.g. a medical/public-health student, a family member working in administration).
Record the real results in the table at the end and summarise them in report Section 7. Do not invent results.

## Set-up (20-25 min per participant)
1. Consent: explain that the data are synthetic, no personal data about the participant are recorded except
   their background (e.g. "public-health student"), and they can stop at any time.
2. Laptop with `run_dashboard.bat` / `make serve` running (opens signed in as national); screen + audio recording only with consent.
3. Think-aloud: ask the participant to say what they are looking for and what they expect.

## Tasks (measure success, time, errors)
| # | Task | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | "Which district needs attention first for dengue right now, and why?" | Uses the ranked table or map markers; mentions the signal/cases |
| T2 | "How many dengue cases do we expect in Dhaka next week, and how sure are we?" | Reads the median and the 80% band from the time series |
| T3 | "When should Chattogram division start its dengue prevention campaign next year?" | Reads the calendar bar / tooltip |
| T4 | "If the budget is cut to 40%, which districts lose most?" | Moves the budget slider and reads the allocation/tiles |
| T5 | "What does '<5' mean on the map?" | Explains suppression for privacy |
| T6 | "Is the latest data fresh?" | Finds a data-freshness stamp |

## Questionnaire
System Usability Scale (10 standard items, 1-5) + 3 open questions: What was confusing? What would you use
this for? What is missing?
